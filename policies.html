Booking policies

The fine print, made simple

Every appointment is a block of time set aside just for your pet. These policies keep things fair for you, for Annee, and for the next pet on her schedule.

Need to cancel or reschedule? Give 24 hours' notice.

Call, text, or message Annee on Facebook at least 24 hours before your appointment. Rescheduling with 24+ hours' notice is always free.

Cancellations and no-shows

  • Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged a late cancellation fee of 45% of the service.
  • If Annee arrives and no one is home or the pet isn't available, the visit counts as a no-show and is charged 50% of the service.
  • Clients with repeat late cancellations or no-shows may be asked to prepay to book again.
  • Life happens. A true emergency (a sick pet, a family emergency, bad weather) is handled case by case. Just reach out as soon as you can.

Getting ready for your appointment

  • Please be home at your appointment time with your pet inside and easy to find.
  • Annee brings everything she needs. You just provide a space to groom, access to water, and an outlet.
  • Take your dog out for a potty break before Annee arrives.
  • Let her know about other pets in the home so the space can stay calm and one-on-one.

Health and vaccinations

  • All dogs and cats must be current on their rabies vaccination.
  • Tell Annee about any health issues, injuries, skin conditions, pregnancy, or senior-pet needs before the groom.
  • If your pet is sick, please reschedule. Annee isn't a vet, and if she spots something concerning during a groom she'll let you know so you can follow up with yours.

Fleas and ticks

  • If fleas or ticks are found, a flea treatment bath is added for $25.
  • Heavy infestations may need to be treated by your vet before the groom can go ahead.

Matted coats

  • Your pet's comfort comes first. Brushing out heavy mats can be painful, so badly matted coats may need to be shaved short to grow back healthy.
  • Extra de-matting time is charged at $15–$20 per 15 minutes.

Behavior and safety

  • Please share any history of biting, scratching, or fear of grooming before your first appointment.
  • If your pet becomes too stressed or the groom becomes unsafe, Annee may stop and finish another day. The time spent is still charged.
  • Annee may decline service when needed to keep a pet, or herself, safe.

Payment

  • Payment is due when the groom is finished.
  • Accepted payment: cash, card, Venmo, or Zelle.

Happy with your groom?

  • If something isn't quite right, let Annee know within 48 hours and she'll make it right.
